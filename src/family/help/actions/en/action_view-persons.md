# Persons

Show or hide the list of persons.

The list is shown in a dock, which can be moved to another side of the window, stacked or tabbed with other docks, or detached into its own window. Selecting a person shows their details, highlights their relations and builds the family tree diagram in the middle of the window around them. The diagram follows whichever list, persons or relations, was used last. Without a single selected person the diagram keeps showing the last one, an empty diagram shows the active person. Double-click a person or relation in the diagram to build the diagram around it and select it in the lists. Controls in the top right corner of the diagram export the displayed tree and change the zoom.

The filter above the list shows only persons containing all typed words. With "Always show highlighted" checked, persons of relations selected in the list of relations stay visible even if they do not match the filter.
