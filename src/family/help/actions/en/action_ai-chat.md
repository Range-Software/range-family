# AI chat

Show or hide the AI chat.

The AI chat is shown in a dock, tabbed with the Cloud AI query. Choose the AI agent and the language of the answer, type a question and press Enter or click "Send". AI agents are configured in the "AI settings manager".

The AI agent receives the family tree as it was when the chat was first shown, without pictures and notes, together with the active person, or your name from the application settings when no person is active. It can answer questions such as "who are my grandparents". When asked to change the family tree, it answers with modified content, the family tree itself is not changed.
